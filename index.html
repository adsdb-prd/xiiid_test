OUR PROJECT

The building blocks
behind better learning.

01 / AI

AI Tutor

Personalized, AI-driven tutoring designed to make high-quality learning more accessible.

AI Tutor adapts to the learner, the curriculum, and the local context—bringing the right explanation, practice, and guidance closer to every student. XIIID is building toward real-world deployment where access to high-quality learning should not depend on income or geography.

02 / AI

AI Studio

An all-in-one AIaaS solution for creating and deploying AI Tutors.

AI Studio turns educational expertise into deployable AI experiences with a modular environment for building, configuring, and scaling AI Tutors. It reduces the distance between a strong idea and a learning service students can actually use.

03 / WEB3

Why Blockchain?

An open network that connects contribution, ownership, and value around AI.

Blockchain gives the ecosystem a transparent way to align creators, educators, learners, and infrastructure. XIIID uses open ownership and incentive mechanisms to help more contributors participate in the future of AI-powered education.
